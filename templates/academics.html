Promotion decisions

Promote to next year

Every enrolled student moves up one class — into the matching stream where one exists (e.g. P.2 A → P.3 A), otherwise into the next plain class. Students marked Repeating or Second sitting stay in their current class, and Discontinued students are unenrolled. Primary 7 students being promoted are marked as having completed primary school. This can be undone afterwards, in whole or for a single student.

Undo last promotion

This moves every student from the last promotion run back to the class they were in before it ran, and restores the decisions that were recorded for them.
